# Going back to stock

We did **not** unjailbreak the Kindle in this project, so this page only summarises what the [kindlemodding.org](https://kindlemodding.org/) guides say. Read the Unjailbreaking and Restoring pages there before relying on it.

- A factory reset alone does **not** remove the jailbreak.
- The documented way is to restore with an official firmware: download the official `.bin` for your exact model (the same version or a newer one) before you start, put it on the Kindle and install it with Update Your Kindle, then factory reset.
- After removing the jailbreak the Kindle can be locked against jailbreaking again. If Amazon later ships a firmware that cannot be jailbroken, you may not be able to redo it. Think before you remove it.
- Keep the backup from step 1. A full system image can only be made after the jailbreak and KUAL are installed, and only on older models.

Download the official firmware and read the restore pages **before** you jailbreak, so the way back is ready.
